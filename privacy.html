Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CineVault handles information when you visit and use our website.

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

At CineVault, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This policy describes the types of information that may be processed through our website, the services we use, and the choices available to visitors.

By using CineVault, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue use of the website.

1. Information We May Process

CineVault is primarily a movie discovery website. Depending on how you use the website and how supporting services operate, certain information may be processed, including:

  • Basic technical information associated with a website request.
  • Browser and device information made available to website infrastructure providers.
  • Information required to deliver website pages and public content.
  • Information voluntarily provided if a contact facility becomes available.

We do not currently provide visitor accounts or a public profile registration system through the main movie discovery interface.

2. Watchlist and Browser Storage

CineVault includes a personal watchlist feature. Saved movie IDs are stored in your browser's Local Storage under the key cinevault_watchlist.

This allows your browser to remember your selected movies when you revisit the website using the same browser and device.

The watchlist is stored locally in your browser rather than being saved as a visitor account in the CineVault movie database.

You can remove saved entries through the website's watchlist controls or clear the relevant browser site data. Clearing browser data may remove your saved watchlist.

3. Cookies and Administrative Sessions

The public movie browsing interface uses browser Local Storage for its watchlist feature.

The administrative area uses a session cookie to support authenticated administrator access. This cookie is intended for website administration and is not a public visitor account feature.

Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers may use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver, secure and operate their services. Their own privacy documentation explains how they handle related information.

4. Cloudflare Workers and D1 Database

CineVault uses Cloudflare infrastructure to operate its website and application programming interfaces. Movie information, collection information and website settings are managed through a Cloudflare D1 database.

When you access the website, your browser may communicate with Cloudflare services to retrieve public content and application data.

Cloudflare may process technical information associated with requests for purposes such as network delivery, reliability, security and abuse prevention, according to its own policies.

Learn more: Cloudflare Privacy Policy .

5. Image Hosting and Cloudinary

CineVault uses Cloudinary to store and deliver website images uploaded by the administrator, such as movie posters, backdrops and other website artwork.

When your browser loads an image hosted by Cloudinary, a request may be sent to Cloudinary to deliver that image. Cloudinary may process technical request information as part of providing its service.

Learn more: Cloudinary Privacy Policy .

6. Google Fonts and External Resources

CineVault may load fonts from Google Fonts to display its website typography. When these resources are requested, your browser connects to Google's servers.

External links may also direct you to websites and services that are not operated by CineVault. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some movie listings may contain links to external websites. These links may include third-party watch, download or other destinations.

If advertising networks, analytics tools or additional third-party services are enabled on CineVault, those providers may process information through their own technologies, subject to their respective policies and applicable requirements.

Third-party advertising and tracking practices are not controlled by this policy. Visitors should review the relevant provider's privacy information and available consent or opt-out choices.

This policy should be updated whenever new advertising, analytics or tracking services are introduced.

8. How Information May Be Used

Information processed through the website and its supporting services may be used where necessary to:

  • Deliver website pages, movie information and images.
  • Remember watchlist selections on your device.
  • Maintain and secure the website infrastructure.
  • Manage administrative access and website content.
  • Diagnose technical errors and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to communications where a contact facility is provided.

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CineVault does not operate a public visitor registration database through its main browsing interface.

Information may nevertheless be processed by service providers involved in website hosting, database operations, image delivery and infrastructure security.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, valid legal process or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or its legitimate interests.

10. Data Retention

Watchlist information remains in your browser's Local Storage until you remove it or clear the relevant browser data.

Information handled by infrastructure and third-party service providers may be retained according to their respective operational, security and legal retention practices.

CineVault does not promise a specific retention period for information maintained by independent service providers.

11. Data Security

We use website infrastructure and administrative access controls intended to help protect the website and its stored content.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage method or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial details or other sensitive information through ordinary public website links.

12. Children's Privacy

CineVault is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a visitor registration system.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through a communication channel should contact the website operator so the matter can be reviewed.

13. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your browser and location, you may be able to:

  • Remove your saved watchlist from the website or browser storage.
  • Manage browser permissions and site data.
  • Use available privacy and tracking controls in your browser.
  • Review the privacy choices offered by third-party services.

Privacy rights and available remedies may also depend on the laws applicable to your location.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information through CineVault, please use the website's Contact page when available.

For privacy-related enquiries, please include enough detail to help identify the issue. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.
