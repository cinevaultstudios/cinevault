Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the CineVault prototype. It must be reviewed and updated to reflect the services, analytics, advertising partners and data practices actually used before public monetisation.

Information stored on your device

The watchlist feature uses your browser's local storage to remember saved titles on that device. This information is not sent to CineVault servers by this prototype.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This prototype does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising networks or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to identify them and explain their data practices and user choices.

Third-party services

Google Fonts may be requested from Google's servers when the site loads. External links may take you to third-party websites governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact

Before publishing this page as a final policy, add a real contact email address controlled by the site owner and obtain appropriate legal review for the countries where the site operates.
